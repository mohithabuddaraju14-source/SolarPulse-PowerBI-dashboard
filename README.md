☀️ SolarPulse – Smart Solar Energy Monitoring Dashboard

Transforming Solar Energy Data into Actionable Insights

About the Project

SolarPulse is a data analytics project focused on exploring solar energy data and transforming it into meaningful visual insights. Using Microsoft Power BI and Excel, the project presents energy trends in an easy-to-understand dashboard format.

The main idea behind SolarPulse is to demonstrate how data visualization can help people understand energy patterns, encourage renewable energy awareness, and support smarter energy-related decisions.

Project Objectives

- Analyze solar energy data to identify meaningful trends and patterns.
- Present key insights through clear and informative visualizations.
- Make energy-related information easier to understand.
- Encourage awareness of renewable energy and sustainable practices.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – For dashboard development and data visualization.
- Microsoft Excel – For dataset organization and data preparation.

Key Highlights

- Data-driven analysis of solar energy information.
- Visual representation of energy trends and insights.
- A dashboard-oriented approach to renewable energy monitoring.
- Focus on sustainability and informed decision-making.
